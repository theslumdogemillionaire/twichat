WHY TWICHAT · 02

Why not
just use the site?

On a stream page, chat sits in a narrow column to the right of the video. In Twichat the proportions are yours: chat takes the window and the stream takes the space you leave it.

This desktop Twitch client keeps several channels open at once without loading Twitch's web page. Chat goes through a single IRC connection over TLS, history stays smooth on busy channels, and switching channels reloads nothing.

THE USUAL STREAM PAGE

Chat fits in a column.

  • Usernames and emotes cut to the bone
  • One browser tab per channel
WITH TWICHAT

Chat takes the window.

  • Chat across the full width, smooth history

  • Up to twenty channels in the same window

UNDER THE HOOD · 03

How it works.

01 / CHAT

IRC, no Twitch page

A single TLS connection serves all your channels. Each channel keeps its last 500 messages, and only the ones on screen are in the DOM.

02 / STREAM

A single HLS stream

Video starts with the channel, resizes, switches to audio only or to its own window, and detects when the stream stops or comes back.

03 / DISPLAY

Avatars and emotes

Twitch avatars are loaded in batches for the authors on screen, along with Twitch, 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ emotes. A mention colours the line and notifies you.

FREQUENTLY ASKED · 04

Before you
download.

Twichat is an independent Twitch client, free and currently in alpha on macOS, Windows and Linux. Here is what to know about IRC chat, the video player, Twitch accounts and supported platforms.

What is Twichat?

Twichat is a desktop Twitch client where chat takes the window. Avatars, badges and emotes show in full, replies form threads, and the stream stays beside it at whatever size you give it.

Can you use Twichat without a Twitch account?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you read channel chat and watch streams. A Twitch account is only needed to send messages and reach authenticated features.

How does Twichat connect to Twitch chat?

Twichat uses a secure IRC connection shared across channels. It does not open Twitch's full web page, which keeps resource use down and chat rendering responsive.

Can you watch a stream inside Twichat?

Yes. An HLS player starts with the channel. It resizes, goes fullscreen, switches to audio only or moves to its own window. One stream is active at a time.

Which avatars and emotes show in chat?

Twichat shows Twitch avatars and badges as well as Twitch, 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ emotes, so each channel keeps its own face.

Which systems is Twichat available on?

Twichat is available for macOS, Windows and Linux. The download offers the build for your system automatically.
